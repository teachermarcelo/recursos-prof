# FLUENT IMMERSION — Production Workflow

## Stage 1 — Pedagogical content
ChatGPT + Word/Google Docs: 01 Overview, 02 Teacher's Guide, 03 Student Book, 04 Student Worksheets, 06 Answer Key, 12 Assessment Pack and 13 Homework & Extension.

## Stage 2 — Language practice
ChatGPT + H5P + audio tool: 07 Vocabulary Lab, 08 Speaking Lab, 09 Reading Lab when applicable, 10 Listening Lab when applicable, 11 Grammar Lab when applicable. Coordinate the Assessment Pack with Stage 1 to avoid conflicting versions.

## Stage 3 — Design and presentation
Canva + PowerPoint: 05 Presentation, covers, flashcards, visuals, print and projection versions. Use original, licensed or public-domain media as appropriate.

## Stage 4 — Quality assurance and delivery
ChatGPT + Word + Drive/Notion: 14 Differentiation & Accessibility, 15 Progress Tracker, 16 Start Here, 17 License & Usage and 18 Quality Assurance Report.

## Publishing flow
1. Create source content.
2. Review CEFR alignment, English, answer keys and accessibility.
3. Export DOCX/PPTX/XLSX/PDF final files.
4. Open and inspect exported files.
5. Complete QA report and version.
6. Separate public, teacher-only and private answer-key materials.
7. Publish the product only after review.

## Notes
- A0 is a curricular pre-A1 introductory stage, not an official standalone CEFR level.
- Do not present an internal quiz as official CEFR certification.
- Do not commit student personal data, secrets or restricted answer keys to the public site.
- Review commercial licenses before selling.
