Travel English:
At the Airport

Aprenda inglês para uma situação real de viagem. Vire flashcards, jogue, descubra pistas, pratique diálogos e conclua o desafio final.

Your flight is boarding!

Find your gate. Check in your bags. Ask politely. Can you complete the airport mission in English?

MISSION 01 · START HERE

Welcome, traveller!

Your mission: check in, understand an airport announcement and ask for what you need in polite English.

🎯 Learning goals

Recognise 8+ airport expressions, find details in a short announcement, make a polite request and complete a check-in role-play.

🧭 How to play

Use the menu to move between missions. Check your answers, collect progress and retry activities. Your progress is stored in this browser.

🎒 Your equipment

Passport, boarding pass, suitcase and a little confidence. No download is required.

🏅 Mission badge

Finish the final quiz and review your result. Try to score 80% or higher.

Imagine you are at an airport. What three things do you need before boarding a plane?

MISSION 03 · MATCHING GAME

Match the airport words.

Select an English expression, then select its Portuguese meaning. Find all the pairs to complete the game.

MISSION 04 · DIALOGUE DETECTIVE

At the check-in desk

Read the dialogue. Spot the useful expressions and then answer the detective questions.

Agent: Good morning. May I see your passport, please?
Passenger: Of course. Here you are.
Agent: Are you checking in any bags?
Passenger: Yes, one suitcase. Could I have a window seat, please?
Agent: Certainly. Here is your boarding pass. Your gate is 18.
Detective question 1: What does the passenger ask for?
Detective question 2: Which expression is a polite request?

MISSION 05 · READING

Gate 18: urgent announcement

Read the airport announcement and scan for important details.

AIRPORT ANNOUNCEMENT

Flight 284 to London

Attention, passengers. Flight 284 to London is now boarding at Gate 18. Please have your boarding pass and passport ready. Passengers who need assistance may speak to an airport staff member. The gate will close 15 minutes before departure. Thank you.

1. What is the destination?
2. Which gate?
3. When will the gate close?
4. Which documents should be ready?

MISSION 06 · SPEAKING ROLE-PLAY

Speak like a confident traveller.

Choose a role and complete the mission. Use the prompt cards as support, then try again without reading.

🧳 Role A · Passenger

You are checking in for a flight. Ask for a window seat, explain you have one suitcase and ask when boarding starts.

Useful language: “Could I have…?”, “I have…”, “What time does boarding start?”

🛂 Role B · Check-in agent

Ask to see the passenger’s passport, ask about bags, offer a seat choice and give Gate 18 as the departure gate.

Useful language: “May I see…?”, “Are you checking in…?”, “Your gate is…”

Build your own dialogue

Uses speech synthesis available in your browser; this is not a prerecorded MP3.

Self-check

MISSION 07 · LISTENING CHALLENGE

Listen for the key details.

Press play to hear the announcement read by your browser. Listen once for the main idea, then again for details.

Listening strategy

First listen: What is happening? Second listen: Which city, gate number and closing time?

MISSION 08 · GRAMMAR BUILDER

Build polite requests.

Use Could I + base verb to ask politely for something.

Could I have…?

Could I have a window seat, please?

Could I change…?

Could I change my seat?

Could I put…?

Could I put my bag here?

Remember

After “could I”, use the base verb: have, change, put.
