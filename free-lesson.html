REAL-LIFE ENGLISH

At the check-in desk

Read the dialogue. Notice the polite questions and useful phrases.

Agent: Good morning. May I see your passport, please?

Passenger: Of course. Here you are.

Agent: Are you checking in any bags?

Passenger: Yes, one suitcase. I also have a carry-on bag.

Agent: Would you prefer an aisle seat or a window seat?

Passenger: A window seat, please, if possible.

Agent: Certainly. Here is your boarding pass. Your flight departs from Gate 12.

Passenger: Thank you. What time does boarding start?

Agent: Boarding starts at 10:20 a.m. Have a nice flight!

Useful phrases

May I see…?Polite request: “Posso ver…?”
Here you are.When you give something to someone.
Would you prefer…?Asking which option someone wants.
If possible.A polite way to say “se possível”.

Comprehension: What is the passenger's departure gate?

READING LAB

Flight information

Read the airport announcement and answer the questions.

FLIGHT 284 — LONDON

Attention, passengers. Flight 284 to London is now boarding at Gate 18. Please have your boarding pass and passport ready. Passengers who need assistance may speak to an airport staff member. The gate will close 15 minutes before departure. Thank you.

1. Where is Flight 284 going?

2. What should passengers have ready?

3. When will the gate close?

SPEAKING LAB

Your turn to speak

Practise aloud. You can work with a partner, your teacher, or record yourself using your device.

Role-play A — Passenger

You are checking in for a flight. Ask for a window seat, say you have one suitcase, and ask when boarding starts.

Useful language: “Could I have…?” · “I have…” · “What time does…?”

Role-play B — Check-in agent

Ask to see the passenger's passport, ask about bags, offer a seat choice, and give a gate number.

Useful language: “May I see…?” · “Are you checking in…?” · “Your gate is…”

Self-check

Teacher tip: For a stronger challenge, change the situation: the flight is delayed, the passenger has excess baggage, or the preferred seat is unavailable.
USEFUL ENGLISH

Polite requests with “Could I…?”

Use Could I…? to make a polite request. Follow it with the base form of the verb.

Could I have a window seat, please?

Could I put my bag here?

Could I change my seat?

Compare: “Give me a window seat.” is direct. “Could I have a window seat, please?” is more polite.

Choose the polite request.
